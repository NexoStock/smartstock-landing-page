Last updated: September 2026

These terms govern the use of SmartStock, the inventory monitoring platform operated by NexoStock. By creating an account or installing a sensor you accept what is written below.

1. The service

SmartStock reads the weight of the products you monitor through IoT sensors, turns it into an estimated stock level, compares it against the quantity you record and notifies you when a product reaches the minimum threshold or when the difference exceeds ten percent. The estimate in units is an aid to decision making, not a legal inventory record.

3. Your data

4. Notifications

Alerts are sent through the channel you configure. Their delivery depends on third-party services and on your connectivity, so we cannot guarantee that every notification arrives instantly. SmartStock does not replace the supervision of your inventory.
